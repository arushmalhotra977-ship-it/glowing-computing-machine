🌌 Discover the language of life

DNA stores genetic instructions in living things. Bioinformatics uses computers to organize and study biological data, including DNA sequences.

🧬

What is DNA?

DNA stands for deoxyribonucleic acid. It carries genetic information used in the growth, development and functioning of living organisms.

🔤

Four DNA bases

A, T, G and C stand for adenine, thymine, guanine and cytosine. Their order forms a DNA sequence.

💻

Bioinformatics

Scientists use computers to compare biological sequences and explore patterns that help investigate biological questions.

🛠️ How does it work?

1

Input

Enter two strings made from A, T, G and C.

2

Validate

The program checks that the bases are valid and both sequences have equal lengths.

3

Compare

It checks each position and counts matching and different bases.

4

Calculate

Similarity (%) = matching positions ÷ total positions × 100.

5

Display

Results appear with colourful highlights so patterns are easy to see.

💡 Quick questions

Does this website need internet?

No. Once saved, this standalone HTML page runs in a modern browser without an API.

Is this a real DNA test?

No. It only compares text sequences and does not analyze a biological sample.

What is the similarity formula?

Similarity (%) = matching positions ÷ total positions × 100.
